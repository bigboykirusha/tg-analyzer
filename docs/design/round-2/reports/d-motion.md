## 1. What is genuinely good (keep it)

- **A real motion token system.** `--duration-press 90 / fast 150 / normal 200 / slow 300 / sheet 400`, `--ease-standard: cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1)` (Emil's strong ease-out), `--ease-drawer` (iOS/Ionic curve), and `--spring-snappy` — Apple's critically damped spring (damping 1, response 0.35) sampled into `linear()` with an `@supports` fallback (`tokens.css:721–746, 808`). This is better than 95 % of web products. No `transition: all`, no `ease-in`, no `scale(0)` anywhere in `app/`.
- **Press feedback is correct where it exists.** `.btn:active` reaches `scale(0.97)` within 40 ms (measured 0.9728 @40 ms, 0.97 @160 ms) on a 90 ms press / 150 ms release — asymmetric, as it should be (`Button.vue:183–186`). Report cards press less (`--press-scale-lg 0.985`). `app.vue:8` adds the empty `touchstart` listener so iOS fires `:active` — a detail most teams miss.
- **Menus are origin-aware and asymmetric**: desktop popover grows from its trigger (`transform-origin: 240px 0` measured), enter 200 ms (opacity 0→1 by ~150 ms, scale 0.97→1), exit 150 ms (`MenuPanel.vue:95–116`). Textbook.
- **Segmented thumb** moves on the compositor with the sampled spring (`SegmentedControl.vue:118–119`); from `touchstart` the ReportNav thumb is already moving at 30 ms — input latency is excellent.
- **Seismograph sweep plays once per session** (`Seismograph.vue:60–76`, sessionStorage) — exactly Emil's frequency rule: a welcome, not a loading ritual. 208 ticks sweep in ~1 s on the first report only.
- **Reduced motion is thought through, not a blanket kill**: movement and scale are dropped, 150 ms fades stay, the spinner keeps turning slower (`base.css:640–661`); sheets become a 150 ms cross-fade without travel (measured: scrim 0→1 in ~130 ms, sheet top fixed at 655). Stories turn off auto-advance instead of racing (`ReportStory.vue:966–971`). Smooth anchor scrolling becomes instant (`ReportNav.vue:29`).
- **Focus ring appears instantly** (`:focus-visible { transition-duration: 0ms }`, measured `td=0s`) and **theme switches in one frame** (`useTheme.ts:28–34`) — correct "don't animate keyboard / system toggles" decisions.
- **Continuity in the loading state**: the report skeleton is already painted in the person's colour with the real name on the poster (`shots/loading-strip.png`, middle frame). That is the right instinct and the seed of a signature transition (§5).
- Bottom sheet has pointer capture, a velocity trail and rubber-band resistance upward (`BottomSheet.vue:41–55`) — the right _ingredients_, even though the release is broken (P1 below).

## 3. Interaction-by-interaction notes

**Route transitions.** There are none (no `pageTransition`, no View Transitions). Home → report is a hard cut to a skeleton, skeleton → content is a second hard cut with a ~45 px layout shift as the poster grows and the ReportNav row appears (`shots/loading-strip.png`). Back navigation restores scroll instantly (y 1500 → 595 at 110 ms) — correct; no smooth-scroll artefact despite `html { scroll-behavior: smooth }`. A hard cut is defensible; what's missing is _spatial continuity_ between the tapped card (avatar + name + person colour) and the poster that inherits exactly those things (§5).

**Scrolling & header.** Scroll itself is native (good: no scroll-jacking, no reveal-on-scroll). The header has three states (clear → poster → glass); the poster→glass seam is the single most visible motion flaw on the main screen (table above). In dark theme the half-state is `rgba(56,17,18,.76)` with blur — a muddy maroon frame for ~200 ms.

**ReportNav.** Thumb latency excellent, spring correct. Bugs: the 900 ms lock bounce; and a 4.5 k px smooth scroll in 1.1 s is a blur of content — for a jump of more than ~2 viewports consider a short fade-out → instant jump → fade-in (HIG: "consider using fades when you need to relocate an object").

**Menus / sheets / dialogs.** Desktop menus: approve. Phone sheets: open curve is good (drawer curve, 852 → 655 px over ~370 ms, settles with the long tail); everything else about the sheet lifecycle needs the fixes above. Enter and exit are symmetric in tokens (`--duration-sheet` both ways) — exit should be ~250 ms.

**Toasts.** Enter 200 ms fade + 8 px drop + 0.97 scale — fine; stacking/move broken; no swipe.

**Buttons / hover / focus.** Approve, with the ChatRow and PinnedPeople/HomeEvent tiles exception: check every tappable non-`.btn` surface for `:active` (ListRow has it; ChatRow does not).

**Loading.** Skeleton shimmer 1.6 s `--ease-loop` in a `translateX` (compositor) — fine. Analysis progress: indeterminate bar → (fake mode) straight to 100 % → row swaps to its idle state in one frame + toast (measured 0 → 173 → 833 → 863 ms). The _completion_ — the emotional peak of the whole product — has no moment.

**«Итоги».** Segment fill: `linear` `scaleX` keyframe, 9 s, paused via `animation-play-state` — correct and cheap. Tap zones 1/3 back, 2/3 forward — correct. Slide change is a hard cut followed by a 400 ms headline rise and an 80 ms-step tile stagger (all tiles in by ~620 ms) — good restraint for 9:16 content. Problems: hold advances (P1), swipe-down dead (P1), chrome does not hide during hold (Instagram fades the bars after ~200 ms so you can read/screenshot), back-tap on slide 1 does nothing instead of restarting the segment, the overlay opens with a pure 200 ms opacity fade (no origin — it should grow from the «Смотреть итоги» preview in the dialog).

**Theme switching.** One-frame swap is honest and avoids half-themed frames. Apple's guidance is to _ease_ large brightness jumps; a 200 ms View-Transition cross-fade of the root (where supported, skipped under RM) would be the polished version. Low priority.

## 4. Verdict (tiers)

1. **Feel-breaking:** stories hold-skips-slide; story swipe-down dead; sheet drag-release freezes and ghosts; ChatRow has no touch-down state; long-press menu likely absent on iOS.
2. **Missed simplifications:** ECharts draw off-screen for everyone and under RM; loading bar lingering at 100 %; `out-in` on login.
3. **Performance:** layout reads on every scroll event in two handlers on the heaviest page; per-frame CSS-variable remap of ~15 tokens on the header subtree at the flip (cheap once, but it toggles every frame when hovering the threshold).
4. **Interruptibility & timing:** sheet close not velocity-aware and symmetric with open; ReportNav lock timer; toasts teleport.
5. **Cohesion:** poster header mixes snapping and tweening properties; ModalDialog-as-sheet vs BottomSheet behave differently; dialog height jumps.
6. **Accessibility:** ECharts ignores RM; RM story bar stuck at 50 %; RM is otherwise above average.

**Decision: Block** (feel-breaking gesture regressions on the core phone flows), though the token foundation is approve-grade.

## 5. Signature interactions (small, purposeful, product-specific)

1. **"The card becomes the poster."** Tap a report card / pinned person → View Transition (`document.startViewTransition`, Nuxt `experimental.viewTransition`) with `view-transition-name` on the avatar and the name: the avatar flies into the header slot, the name scales up into the display name, the person's colour floods from the card's rect to the full poster (clip-path inset from the card bounds, 350 ms `--ease-drawer`). Back reverses it. It explains _where the report came from_ and turns the existing colour continuity into a felt one. RM → 150 ms cross-fade. Fallback → today's cut.
2. **Scroll-linked large title.** The poster name compresses into the toolbar: header colour, title opacity and a 4 px rise driven by the last 40 px of the poster's travel (scroll-driven animation, no JS per frame). Same tokens, no new decoration — just the HIG pattern done continuously instead of as a switch.
3. **The seismograph is the progress bar.** While a chat is analysed, the row (and the header activity pill) draws the seismograph ticks as months are scanned — the indeterminate bar becomes the chat's actual pulse appearing. On completion, the ticks settle and the row's subtitle cross-fades to «4 800 сообщений»; the toast is optional. One authored moment at the emotional peak, reusing the brand mark.
4. **Hold-to-read stories.** Hold pauses _and_ fades the chrome (bars, close) to 0 after 200 ms; release resumes without navigating; the frame follows a downward drag 1:1 with slight scale-down (0.94 at 200 px) and dismisses on velocity > 0.11 px/ms or 25 % height, springing back otherwise (damping 1, response 0.3).
5. **Lift on long-press.** Chat rows (and report cards) scale to 0.98 with a soft shadow during the 450 ms hold, then the sheet rises _from the row_ (origin at the row, not a generic bottom sheet slide) — the Telegram feel the code comment promises.
6. **Number ticker for one figure only.** «79 / 21» and the poster's message count roll up once (tabular nums already in place, 600 ms ease-out) on the first report view of a session, gated like the seismograph. Not on every card — one number per report.

## 6. What to reconsider in the Portrait system (motion lens)

- **Poster → report transition** is the system's strongest idea visually and its weakest kinetically: the poster is a static slab that the header _switches_ away from. Make the poster the hinge of continuity (signatures 1–2).
- **Header/navigation**: the three-state header is right; its implementation as a class toggle is not. Treat it as one continuous material.
- **Mobile feels compressed, not designed, in motion**: sheets fade like desktop modals, dialogs are sheets without sheet physics, gestures are emulated on release instead of tracked. The desktop layer (menus, presses, segmented thumb) is genuinely designed; the touch layer is not yet.
- **7 Telegram colour families** are an asset for motion: colour is identity, so colour-flood transitions carry meaning, not decoration.
- **Onest/neutral surfaces**: no motion objection.
